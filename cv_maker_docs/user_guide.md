# CV Maker - User Guide

Welcome to the **Raynova Solution** AI CV Maker! This guide will provide step-by-step instructions on how to use the platform to generate a highly professional resume, CV, or cover letter instantly.

## 1. Navigating the Interface
When you launch the application, your screen is divided into three primary segments:
1. **Left Sidebar Navigation:** Here you can select the **Document Type** (Professional Resume, Academic CV, or Cover Letter).
2. **Middle Form Pane:** The main interaction area where you fill out your information.
3. **Right Live Preview (Desktop Only):** Where your final document renders in real-time.

*(On mobile devices, you can toggle between the Editor and the Preview using the Eye icon in the top header).*

## 2. Generating Your Document
1. **Select Document Type:** Click on your preferred option from the left sidebar.
2. **Fill in Details:** Enter all the necessary fields inside the builder section. Try to be as descriptive as possible so the AI can capture your intent accurately.
3. **Click Generate:** Press the "Generate with AI" button. The system will process your information and intelligently rewrite and format it.
4. **Review Results:** Look at the right-side panel (or toggle to the preview screen on mobile). Your finished document will appear fully laid out.

## 3. Customizing the Design
You have full creative control over how your document looks:
- **Colors:** At the bottom of the left sidebar, choose a primary color theme (e.g., Blue, Rose, Emerald). Play around to see what suits your personality.
- **Layout:** Switch between a 'Sidebar' style layout or a 'Minimalist' modern approach.
- **Background Details:** Choose between 'Glassmorphism' (modern, translucent layered design) or 'Solid' backgrounds.
- **Dark/Light Mode:** In the top right header area, you can toggle the color theme of the editor interface itself.

## 4. Exporting Your Work
Once you are 100% satisfied with the generated print and layout:
1. **Download PDF:** Click the `Download PDF` button situated in the top right control bar. The system will convert your HTML design to a high-quality, ATS-friendly PDF file instantly.
2. **Copy Source:** Click the `Source` (Copy) button to grab the raw generated HTML. This is helpful if you want to paste the code onto a personal portfolio website.

## Troubleshooting
- **Missing Information:** If some details aren't appearing, ensure you filled the text boxes completely before clicking generate.
- **PDF Formatting Issues:** If the downloaded PDF looks slightly different, ensure your browser has finished loading custom fonts and icons before clicking download. Try downloading again after waiting 2-3 seconds.
